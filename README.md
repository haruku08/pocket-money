# Pocket Money

A small app to see where pocket money goes: PostFinance and Revolut balances, scooter gas, parking, paying off the license debt, job income, and a "what if" planner.

Open it at **https://haruku08.github.io/pocket-money/**

Everything is saved in the browser on your own phone. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use **Setup → Copy backup code** to keep a copy.
